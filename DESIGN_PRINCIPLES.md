# Noodle Morphs Design Principles

## Core Design Philosophy

**Noodle Morphs** is a calm, zero-failure ball python vivarium and genetic breeding experience. It prioritizes meaningful morph discovery, gentle experimentation, and long-term lineage progression over dumping its entire 21-gene library on the player at once. The underlying Mendelian genetics, modular skin pattern shader rules, and vigour systems can be rich and authentic without forcing players to study Punnett squares on minute one.

The objective is to sustain anticipation for the next clutch—wondering whether an egg will finally hatch a visual recessive, a clean Super complex, or an unexpected mutation—rather than exhausting the most exciting morphs in the opening ten minutes.

## Less Is More: Complexity vs. Depth

**Complexity** is the amount of information a player must track. **Depth** is the number of meaningful decisions they can make with that information.

A curated ball python gene pool generates enormous emergent depth through recessive carriers (`Het`), co-dominant Super complexes, modular skin patterns (such as `(lines) (thin) (dotted)`), vigour recovery pacing, and selective reinvestment.

- **Minimize extraneous mechanics:** Keep one clean whole-dollar (`$`) currency, zero failure states, and zero punitive maintenance timers.
- **Maximize interactions between existing systems:** Vigour links breeding pace, recovery rest, and morph traits (like Spider's upside-down posture and lower vigour cap); selling links First-Sale discovery bonuses to unlocking **The Hiss Depot** and funding **The Jean Pool**.
- **Introduce new systems only when they create real choices:** Every knob—like the settable Incubator clutch/time dial or the self-balancing Gene Inheritance Pie—exists to let players steer strategy, not add busywork.
- **Prefer emergent combinations over special-case rules:** Combining `Pastel`, `Enchi`, `Banana`, `Fire`, `Yellow Belly`, `Black Pastel`, and recessives naturally creates thousands of visual morphs and distinct procedural patterns without hardcoding every combo.
- **Make genetic mastery rewarding, never mandatory:** Players who simply pair their prettiest noodles still hatch delightful morphs, while players who track `Het` carriers, undiscovered offspring counts, and Super complexes unlock rare outcomes much faster.

A calm vivarium with richly interlocking genetics is always preferable to a cluttered dashboard with obvious upgrades.

## Progressive Disclosure

Systems in **Noodle Morphs** surface naturally as the player's collection matures, teaching through observation inside the terrarium and clutch reveal rather than upfront walls of text:

1. **Tactile Vivarium & Basic Inheritance:** Meet your founding pair, watch them slither, pet them, and hatch your first 2-egg clutch to see dominant/co-dominant visual traits mix.
2. **Hidden Recessive Genetics (`Het`):** Notice your founding pair shares a hidden `Het` trait and watch a rare `1 in 16` visual recessive (like *Albino*, *Piebald*, *Clown*, or *Hypo*) emerge from two non-visual parents.
3. **First Sale & Vivarium Hardware:** Sell a hatchling (earning an obvious **First Sale of Morph Bonus**), unlocking **The Hiss Depot** and giving you funds to consider your first upgrade or new gene.
4. **Expanding the Gene Pool:** Visit **The Jean Pool** to adopt single-missing-gene breeders (one per shipment on a cooldown) and deliberately widen your lineage toward all 21 real-world ball python traits.
5. **Multi-Generation Projects & Supers:** Pair Gen 2 offspring to unlock iconic ball python **Supers & Complexes** (*Blue-Eyed Leucistic*, *Black-Eyed Leucistic*, *Ivory*, *8-Ball*, *Super Pastel*) and multi-gene stacks.
6. **Lineage Optimization:** Balance vigour recovery across multiple pairs, tune **The Hiss Depot**'s Incubator Capacity Dial (`2e/2m` up to `6e/6m`), expand terrarium glass display slots, and shape inheritance odds with the Self-Balancing Gene Inheritance Pie.

Progressive disclosure guides newcomers gently without gating experienced breeders who already know how to read `Het` lines and Punnett previews.

## Preserve Future Discoveries

Morph discoveries are **Noodle Morphs**'s primary reservoir of delight. Handing out legendary complexes in the opening five minutes weakens the thrill of earning them across generations.

Key milestone moments spaced naturally across a playthrough include:

- **First combined visual morph** (e.g., *Banana Enchi* or *Pastel Cypress*).
- **First visual recessive** hatched from `Het × Het` parents (*Albino*, *Piebald*, *Clown*, *Hypo*).
- **First spontaneous mutation** bringing a brand-new locus into the vivarium.
- **First Super or Complex** (*Blue-Eyed Leucistic*, *Black-Eyed Leucistic*, *Ivory*, *8-Ball*, *Super Pastel*).
- **First three-gene+ combination** with layered modular pattern rules.
- **First double recessive** (e.g., *Albino Piebald* or *Lavender Albino Clown*).
- **First rare late-game recessive** (*Lavender Albino*, *Desert Ghost*, *Ultramel*) acquired via **The Jean Pool** or mutation.
- **First high-payout jackpot hatchling** triggering a major First-Sale Morph Bonus.

The opening pair should immediately demonstrate the charm of Mendelian breeding without spending these late-game fireworks early.

## Controlled Randomness ("Classic 2+1" Curated Starts)

Randomness should give every new vivarium its own personality and story—never a crippled start.

**Noodle Morphs** uses the hardened **Classic 2+1** starter recipe with a subtle inline `reroll` link for returning players:

- **Two distinct, healthy visual genes** (one guaranteed **Co-dominant** so a Super morph is always achievable by Gen 2, plus a second distinct healthy visual gene).
- **No starting handicaps:** Low-vigour neurological traits like `Spider` (80 max vigour, upside-down posture) are excluded from starter rolls so players choose to adopt them intentionally later.
- **No premature Super shortcuts:** Allelic twins (`Mojave × Lesser`) are kept apart on the starter roll so *Blue-Eyed Leucistic* stays an earned milestone rather than an accidental Clutch #1 drop.
- **One shared opening recessive `Het`** (`Albino`, `Piebald`, `Clown`, or `Hypo`), guaranteeing every fresh run begins with **exactly 8 Mendelian visual outcomes (6 undiscovered)** including a `1 in 16` jackpot, while reserving *Lavender Albino*, *Desert Ghost*, and *Ultramel* for later discovery.

*Randomize the noodles, curate the genetics.*

## Meaningful Decisions and Trade-Offs

**Noodle Morphs** avoids linear "always click the cheapest upgrade" optimization by creating genuine tension across choices:

- **Keep vs. Sell:** Selling a newly hatched morph immediately claims the **First Sale of Morph Bonus** to fund new genes or hardware, whereas keeping it preserves a key breeder or rare carrier (backed by prominent **Last-Two Lineage Warnings** so players never sell a vital gene by accident).
- **Three Competing Investment Pillars:**
  1. **Possibility — The Jean Pool:** Expands your genetic reach one missing locus at a time, gated by escalating prices and a `5×` shipment cooldown.
  2. **Capacity — Terrarium Glass Expansion:** Permanently raises how many ball pythons lounge together on display in your live terrarium.
  3. **Tuning & Throughput — The Hiss Depot:** Unlocks a *settable* Incubator range (`+1 egg / +1 min` per tier, never forcing longer waits) and raises caps on the **Self-Balancing Gene Inheritance Pie** (where boosting Dominant, Co-dominant, or Recessive automatically trims the other two so total weight stays `100%`).
- **Vigour & Rotation:** Breeding drains vigour (5 baseline clutches before resting; lower-vigour lines like *Spider* need rest sooner) and scales hatch time from `0.5×` to `2×`, rewarding players who rotate rested pairs instead of overworking a single duo.

## Every Hatchling Should Have Potential

Not every egg needs to hatch a legendary double recessive. Even a humble *Normal* or duplicate *Pastel* should advance your vivarium:

- **Immediate sale funds** (especially if it triggers a **First Sale of Morph Bonus**).
- **Hidden `Het` carrying potential** for future recessive projects.
- **Fresh full vigour (`100%`)** ready to step in while older parents recover.
- **Morphdex & lineage records** enriching your vivarium's family tree and custom-named favorites.

An ordinary hatchling should never feel like a dead roll.

## Discovery as a Primary Reward

Currency (`$`), vigour pacing, and shop hardware exist purely to support the quiet joy of watching your noodles slither and cracking open the next clutch:

> **Breed → Hatch → Discover → Keep or Sell → Reinvest → Breed**

Every screen—from the live procedural terrarium to the card-flip Clutch Reveal and Morphdex—keeps the snakes themselves front and center.

## Starting Configuration Comparison

| Starting Configuration | Strength | Trade-Off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minimalist (1 visual + Normal)** | Ultra-simple first clutch; huge discovery runway. | Slow initial novelty before first combo. |
| **Classic 2+1 (Noodle Morphs Default)** | Guarantees 6 undiscovered morphs (8 outcomes), Gen-2 Super potential, healthy vigour, and clean `Het` discovery. | Introduces hidden `Het` inheritance early. |
| **Double-Stacked (2×1 + Shared Het)** | Rich 16-outcome Clutch #1 with rapid First-Sale bonuses. | Front-loads rewards and reduces early urgency to visit The Jean Pool. |
| **Super-Ready (Shared Codom + 2 Unique)** | Every gene is visible immediately; `25%` Super chance on Clutch #1. | Spends the "First Super" milestone immediately. |
| **Strict Zero-Overlap (2 Visuals + 2 Different Hets)** | Maximum gene diversity from minute one; deep Gen 2 puzzle. | Only 4 outcomes on Clutch #1; slower early momentum. |

## Economic and Progression Balance

Starter genetics, valuation multipliers, First-Sale Bonuses, **The Jean Pool** price escalation, and **The Hiss Depot**'s independent exponential upgrade curves are tuned as one interconnected ecosystem:

- **If early payouts are too generous:** Players empty **The Jean Pool** on cooldown without tough keep-or-sell choices, and upgrades lose strategic weight.
- **If early payouts are too tight:** Players feel stuck waiting on duplicate clutches before unlocking **The Hiss Depot** or affording their first missing gene.
- **Target feel:** Your very first clutch should yield enough from selling one hatchling (with First-Sale Bonus) to unlock **The Hiss Depot** and immediately make a meaningful choice between adopting a new gene from **The Jean Pool** or investing in your first incubator/bias upgrade.

## Playtesting and Evaluation

Balance and UX changes in **Noodle Morphs** should be judged by real session feel rather than theoretical combo math alone:

- **Time to first new morph discovery** and **time to first visual recessive**.
- **Time to first sale** (unlocking **The Hiss Depot**) and **time to first Jean Pool adoption**.
- **Frequency of genuine keep-or-sell hesitation** (especially when a First-Sale Bonus collides with a Last-Two Lineage Warning).
- **Clarity of breeding vigour** and how naturally players rotate resting pairs via `[Change Pair]`.
- **Usefulness of every investment path** (Incubator Dial vs. Display Slots vs. Gene Inheritance Pie vs. Jean Pool).
- **Reroll frequency** on the Founding Pair modal (ensuring starts feel consistently inviting rather than baiting resets).

## Noodle Morphs Design Pillars

1. **Easy to enjoy, rewarding to master.**  
   Watching calm noodles slither and cracking eggs is instantly cozy; mastering `Het` lines, Supers, and vigour rotation deepens the reward.

2. **Discovery drives the vivarium.**  
   Dollars, shops, and upgrades exist to unlock new morph possibilities—never to overshadow the snakes.

3. **Every snake has potential.**  
   Beauty on display, sale payout, hidden `Het` genetics, and fresh breeding vigour are distinct reasons to value a hatchling.

4. **Controlled randomness creates stories, not handicaps.**  
   Curated Classic 2+1 starts and fair odds make each vivarium unique without punishing bad luck.

5. **Meaningful trade-offs over feature bloat.**  
   Deepen how vigour, genetics, selling, and shop tuning interact before adding new currencies or rules.

6. **Always preserve anticipation.**  
   Whether it is an unseen `???` slot in your Punnett preview, a `1 in 16` recessive, a Super complex, or the next **Jean Pool** shipment, there should always be an exciting hatch just around the corner.

## Guiding Question

Before adding any new morph, shop upgrade, or mechanic to **Noodle Morphs**, ask:

> *Does this create a meaningful decision and sustain anticipation for the next clutch, or does it just hand out more content sooner?*
